Figure 1 | Evidence-grounded A2G-MambaKT architecture. Shifted target and strictly historical interactions are represented by item, concept, and response embeddings. Training-fold empirical item/concept priors and causal recency-weighted concept evidence are fused with six retained statistics before projection to a 256-dimensional token. The executed backbone applies a boundary-aware selective state-space recurrence (global scope weight 1.0; local floor 0.5), followed by four causal post-normalization attention blocks; blocks 1-3 include MHA+FFN and block 4 is MHA-only with eight heads. The prediction MLP is combined with direct prior and statistic logit residuals using scales 0.08 and 0.12, then passed through sigmoid for next-response prediction. The highlighted Selective-SSM is the only module with strict matched AUC/ACC-only 5/5 ablation support under the frozen protocol. Full-model evidence is 6/8 baseline-positive datasets. All results shown are fold0/seed42 validation-only supporting evidence; test/window-test access is false, quality_freeze=false, and no universal-SOTA, five-fold, or multi-seed claim is made. Train-only item residual dropout=0.4 is shown as a dashed intervention and does not add parameters or state keys.
